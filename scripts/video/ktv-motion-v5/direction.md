# KTV V5 — Pulse of Kedah

Fresh concept. Do not reuse V3/V4 composition language.

## Core idea
Kedah already has talent, opportunities and institutions. The film visualises disconnected signals becoming one coordinated pulse through Kedah Tech Valley.

## Visual rules
- No floating information cards.
- No three-column photo wall.
- No “six nodes around a circle” composition.
- One visual idea per shot.
- Oversized editorial typography.
- Motion should feel continuous: pulse → interference → sweep → sync → bloom → resolve.
- Use real KTV assets only for proof/brand moments.

## Brand
- Ink #050806
- Paper #EEF2EA
- Green #B9F05D
- Gold #E3BB55
- Cyan #82D8CB
- Violet #B9A5FF

## Sound
Text-led film. No narration in V5. Motion effects and low restrained synthesized tonal bed only. Sound must support visible events, not fill every second.
