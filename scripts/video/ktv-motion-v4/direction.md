# Direction

## Facts
- Kedah Tech Valley connects technology professionals, founders, students, communities and organisations so previously separated talent becomes visible to one another. — source: taufec/kedah-digital/app/page.tsx, Platform Komuniti
- KTV connects industry needs, academia and agencies so ideas, talent and opportunities do not move separately. — source: taufec/kedah-digital/app/page.tsx, Industri × Akademia × Agensi
- KTV describes itself as connective tissue that organises relationships between community, companies, agencies and institutions into a more directed movement. — source: taufec/kedah-digital/app/page.tsx, Ekosistem Teknologi Strategik
- The site CTA is “Jadi rakan strategik”. — source: taufec/kedah-digital/app/page.tsx
- Brand palette is ink #050806, paper #EEF2EA, green #B9F05D, gold #E3BB55, cyan #82D8CB and violet #B9A5FF. — source: taufec/kedah-digital/app/globals.css
- All photography, strategy art, logos and the supplied 25.07s Malay narration are existing KTV material. — source: project assets/ and Ajint preflight

## Brief
- **Asked for:** guna skill ni tengok utk generate video
- **Product and promise:** Kedah Tech Valley is the connective tissue that turns separated technology actors into a more directed ecosystem.
- **Format:** 1920x1080 @ 30fps, 26s, Malay on-screen copy.
- **Assets:** KTV logo/symbol, community photography, strategy art and existing Malay voice-mix.wav.
- **Subject:** The real KTV community and ecosystem represented by supplied KTV assets.
- **Invented:** None; layout, motion and abstract connection lines are editorial treatment only.

## Concept
- **Options:** A) scattered ecosystem nodes physically connect into the KTV mark; B) documentary photo mosaic with kinetic chapter typography; C) strategic sketchnote diagrams morph into live community photography.
- **Chosen:** A, because the motion itself demonstrates KTV’s core “connective tissue” idea instead of merely describing it.
- **Copy system:** Short chapter statements plus labels; the narration carries detail.

## Look
- **Frame and palette:** Dark KTV ink world with paper type and real green/gold/cyan/violet accents sampled from the website.
- **Type:** Bundled Inter for clean motion readability; logo retains official brand artwork.
- **World:** Start in a photographic Kedah community world, break into separated ecosystem nodes, draw connections into KTV, then resolve into real community proof and a calm CTA.

## Sound
- **Mode:** Existing user narration plus motion effects; no added music.
- **Concept:** Restrained glass/synth transition cues tied to visible connection/reveal moments; narration stays dominant.
- **Arc:** 0.48 hook → 0.58 tension → 0.86 connection payoff → 0.72 human proof → 0.55 calm CTA.
